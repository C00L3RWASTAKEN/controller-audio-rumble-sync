Controller Rumble Sync

Load any audio file and your controller rumbles to it. Bass drives the left motor, treble drives the right. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Press any button on your controller to connect it.

Left motorbass
Right motortreble

Raise the audio delay if the rumble feels late compared with the sound. Wireless controllers usually need 50–150 ms.

Rumble through the browser works in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave, not Firefox or Safari. Playback pauses if you switch tabs, so the rumble can’t get stuck on.
